🐣 Adopt a real one on XRPL mainnet
or adopt without connecting a wallet
Paste any XRPL address. We mint your Ledgerling on XRPL mainnet, recorded to your address, and anyone can verify its whole life from the ledger. The NFT is held by the game issuer for now, because the issuer is what updates it as your pet lives. No wallet connection and nothing to install. Once you have one, feeding it and fighting on the ladder are real transactions you approve in Xaman.
⚔️ Ladder — fight a published opponent
Their stats are public before you fight. The dice are the hash of a ledger that has not closed yet, so nobody can know or rig the result. Free, and you can re-check it afterwards.
🥊 Fight Club — pet vs pet
Start a fight and share the battle id, or paste one somebody sent you. Free — 1 drop from each side, settled on the ledger. The dice are the hash of a ledger that has not closed yet when the second pet commits, so nobody can know or rig the result.
👻 Fight a ghost — no waiting
Fight a snapshot of somebody else's pet. They don't need to be online, or to agree, and nothing lands on their record — only yours. Same dice as everything else: the hash of a ledger that hasn't closed yet.
